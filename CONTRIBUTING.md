# Feedback

git-parity is developed by its author alone. **Pull requests are not accepted**, and the
license does not allow publishing modified versions.

Feedback is very welcome:

- **Bugs:** open an [issue](https://github.com/Dev-Wesley-13/git-parity/issues) with the report
  git-parity printed, your agent and its version, your operating system, and `git --version`.
- **Ideas and requests:** open an issue describing what you need and why.
- **Security problems:** do not open a public issue; follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- **Permission to modify or redistribute:** open an issue or contact the author through GitHub.
